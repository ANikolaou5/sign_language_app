Delete Your SiLAc Account

This page explains how users of SiLAc, developed by RayLabz, can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to raylabzg@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request – SiLAc.
  3. Include the email address associated with your SiLAc account.
  4. We may contact you to verify ownership of the account before deletion.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Once your request has been verified, we will permanently delete:

  • Your user account.
  • Your username.
  • Your email address.
  • Any data associated with your account stored by SiLAc in Firebase Firestore.

What Data May Be Retained?

We do not retain copies of your personal account information after deletion, except where retention is required by applicable law or to resolve legal obligations, disputes, or security incidents.

If any such retention is required, the information will be retained only for the minimum period required by law and then permanently deleted.

Processing Time

Verified account deletion requests are normally processed within 30 days.
