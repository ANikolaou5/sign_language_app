Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RayLabz ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the SiLAc mobile application.

By using SiLAc, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

SiLAc collects only the information required to authenticate users.

The information collected includes:

  • Username
  • Email address

No additional personal information is intentionally collected. SiLAc does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, location information, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

2. Authentication

User authentication is provided through Firebase Authentication. Authentication information is used solely for creating and managing user accounts and securely signing users into the application.

3. Data Storage

User account information is stored securely using Google Firebase Firestore. Appropriate technical and organizational measures are implemented to protect stored information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate your identity.
  • Provide access to the application's features.
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the service.

We do not sell, rent, share, or otherwise disclose your personal information for marketing or advertising purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

SiLAc uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Google Cloud Firestore

These services process data only as necessary to provide authentication and secure storage functionality.

6. Analytics

SiLAc does not use analytics services, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, or profiling tools.

7. Data Retention

Your account information is retained for as long as your account remains active or as required to comply with applicable legal obligations.

8. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located within the European Economic Area (EEA), you may have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Request access to your personal data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Request restriction of processing where applicable.
  • Object to processing where permitted by law.
  • Request a copy of your personal data.

Requests relating to your personal information may be submitted using the contact details below.

9. Account Deletion

Users cannot currently delete their accounts directly within the application. If you wish to request deletion of your account or associated personal data, please contact us using the email addresses provided below.

10. Children's Privacy

SiLAc is not intended for children under the age required by applicable data protection laws without appropriate parental or guardian consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by law.
